# Mission

**Caskade is the best game launcher available: every game you own, on every device in your home, in one library, one press from playing.**

PC gamers have games spread across a dozen stores and launchers, emulators that each need configuring, ROM folders, web games, fan ports and the copies on the other PC in the house. Every launcher holds part of the collection, and most want an account, a connection or a subscription to show you even that part. Caskade puts all of it in one place and does the plumbing for you.

## What we believe

1. **One library for everything.** Store games, launcher games, emulated games, web games, fan ports and games streamed from another device are all games. They share one library, one search, one Play button and one count of playtime.
2. **It's yours, and it works offline.** No Caskade account, no telemetry, no advertising. The library lives on your device. Everything that doesn't need the internet works without it: discovery, launching, emulation, sync between your own devices, streaming on your own network. Caches are kept when you go offline, never thrown away.
3. **The plumbing is invisible.** Caskade installs and configures emulators, BIOS files, Proton, streaming hosts and clients so you don't have to. You switch something on and it works. You never see Sunshine, Moonlight, a config file or a terminal unless you go looking.
4. **Nothing happens behind your back.** Nothing downloads, installs or runs until you confirm it. An edit you make is never undone by a refresh. Removing something from Caskade keeps your files unless you said otherwise. Updates are offered, never forced.
5. **The household, not the individual.** Several people share a PC and several PCs share a home. Accounts with age limits and PINs, devices that pair and keep the library in step, saves that follow the player, games copied over the local network and played from the next room.
6. **Desk and couch are equal.** The same app works with a mouse and keyboard on a desktop and with a pad on a TV, a handheld or a Steam Deck. Neither is an afterthought.
7. **Open to change.** Themes restyle all of it and plugins extend it, through public, versioned contracts. Caskade is open source under the MIT licence and runs on Windows, Linux and macOS.

## What Caskade is not

- **Not a store.** Caskade sells nothing and adds no DRM. It links to stores and reads the libraries you already own.
- **Not a service.** There is no Caskade cloud. Devices talk to each other directly. The only server Caskade runs answers game-information questions (IGDB metadata) and learns nothing about who asked.
- **Not a replacement for the programs it drives.** Emulators, launchers, Sunshine and Moonlight remain separate programs, used as their licences allow.

## How we judge our work

- A new user with games spread across stores, emulators and devices sees all of them, playable, within minutes of first start, without reading anything.
- Pressing Play works the first time: the right program, the right settings, the save from the other PC, playtime counted.
- A library of 10,000 games stays fast and light.
- It still works on a plane.
